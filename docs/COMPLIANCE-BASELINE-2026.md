# Luxmi InvestCare — Compliance Baseline

**Business:** Luxmi InvestCare  
**Status:** AMFI-Registered Mutual Fund Distributor  
**ARN:** 365140  
**Website:** https://luxmiinvestcare.com

## Positioning

The public website should consistently identify Luxmi InvestCare as an AMFI-Registered Mutual Fund Distributor and present services as mutual fund distribution, investor education, product information and transaction/process support.

Do not present Luxmi InvestCare as an Investment Adviser, financial planner, wealth manager or portfolio manager unless a separate applicable registration authorizes that activity.

## Calculator language

Use:
- Illustrative Return Assumption
- Illustrative Future Value
- Illustrative Investment Amount
- One-Time Investment Amount
- Monthly SIP Amount
- Annual SIP Increase
- Investment Period

Avoid using calculator labels that imply a personalized recommendation, assurance or guaranteed outcome, including:
- Expected Return
- Guaranteed Return
- Required Monthly SIP (when presented as a prescribed personal amount)
- Wealth Created
- Guaranteed/Assured Corpus
- Retirement Planner
- Goal Planner as a personalized planning service

## Educational positioning

Use:
- Investor Education
- Investment Illustration
- Education Cost Illustration
- Understanding Mutual Funds
- Understanding SIP/STP/SWP
- Investor Support

## Advertising

Scheme-specific advertisements, performance claims, AMC logos/material, testimonials and social-media recommendations require separate review against the applicable SEBI/AMFI requirements and, where applicable, AMC approval. A disclaimer must not be treated as a cure for misleading or non-compliant substantive claims.

## Important

This file is an implementation baseline, not a legal opinion or substitute for review of the specific communication, product, registration status and applicable current SEBI/AMFI circulars.
